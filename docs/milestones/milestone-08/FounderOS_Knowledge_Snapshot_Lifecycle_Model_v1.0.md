    Created

    Validated

    Reviewing
     |
     v
    Approved
     |
     v
    Active
     |
     v
    Superseded
     |
     v
    Archived

## State Definitions

Created: Snapshot generated from a corpus state.

Validated: Passed schema, provenance, and integrity checks.

Reviewing: Human review in progress.

Approved: Ready for activation.

Active: Current operational knowledge state.

Superseded: Replaced by a newer active snapshot.

Archived: Historical record preserved.

## Principle

Knowledge state transitions require explicit governance.
