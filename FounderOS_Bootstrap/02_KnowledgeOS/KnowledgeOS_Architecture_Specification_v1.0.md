# 1. Purpose

KnowledgeOS is the intelligence foundation layer of FounderOS.

Its mission:

> Transform fragmented founder knowledge into structured, connected, and
> actionable intelligence.

KnowledgeOS is responsible for:

-   capturing knowledge
-   structuring information
-   preserving decisions
-   maintaining relationships
-   providing context to AI agents

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Design Philosophy

## Knowledge Is Not Storage

Traditional knowledge management:

    Document

    ↓

    Search

    ↓

    Read

KnowledgeOS:

    Knowledge

    ↓

    Structure

    ↓

    Relationship

    ↓

    Context

    ↓

    Decision Intelligence

The goal is not storing more information.

The goal is producing better intelligence.

# 5. KnowledgeOS Architecture Layers

## Layer 1 --- Human Knowledge Layer

Purpose:

Human-owned source of truth.

Technology:

-   Obsidian
-   Markdown
-   Git version control

Contains:

-   ideas
-   research
-   decisions
-   principles
-   projects

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Layer 2 --- Knowledge Processing Layer

Purpose:

Convert human knowledge into machine-readable intelligence.

Pipeline:

    Markdown

    ↓

    Parser

    ↓

    Metadata Extraction

    ↓

    Knowledge Object

    ↓

    Indexing

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Layer 3 --- Intelligence Layer

Purpose:

Enable AI understanding.

Components:

-   Vector retrieval
-   Knowledge graph
-   Context assembly

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Layer 4 --- Agent Context Layer

Purpose:

Provide information to AI agents.

Hermes asks:

"Should Adam build this startup idea?"

KnowledgeOS provides:

    Founder Profile

    +

    Current Projects

    +

    Past Decisions

    +

    Market Research

    +

    Previous Lessons

# 8. KnowledgeOS Principles

## Principle 1 --- Human Source of Truth

AI processes knowledge.

AI does not own knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 2 --- Structure Before Intelligence

Unstructured information creates weak AI output.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 3 --- Context Before Response

AI should retrieve before reasoning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 4 --- Decisions Are First-Class Knowledge

A decision without reasoning is incomplete.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 5 --- Knowledge Must Compound

Every project should improve future projects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. KnowledgeOS Success Criteria

KnowledgeOS succeeds when FounderOS can answer:

## Founder Questions

-   Why did I choose this?
-   Have I seen this pattern before?
-   What did I learn previously?

## Project Questions

-   What is the current status?
-   What risks exist?
-   What decisions were made?

## Strategic Questions

-   Should I pursue this opportunity?
-   What should I prioritize?
